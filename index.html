The science, clearly

What each ingredient
brings to the formula.

Beginning with the ingredients people know best, then moving into the compounds at the centre of current healthy-ageing research.

Mg

01 · Essential mineral

Magnesium

Magnesium helps hundreds of enzyme systems work and is required for energy production, muscle function and nerve signalling.

NIH fact sheet ↗

02 · Botanical

Ashwagandha

Some preparations may help with stress and sleep. Human studies are often small and use different preparations, so the findings need careful interpretation.

NCCIH evidence summary ↗

03 · Root + extract

Turmeric + black pepper

Turmeric contains curcuminoids. Piperine from black pepper is often paired with curcumin to increase its bioavailability; clinical evidence remains mixed.

NCCIH evidence summary ↗

04 · Mushroom extract

Lion’s Mane

Early human research has explored mood and cognitive function. Results from intervention studies are mixed, and larger trials are still needed.

Human evidence review ↗
NRNAD+

05 · Cellular cofactor research

Nicotinamide Riboside + NAD

NR is a precursor the body can use to make NAD+, a cofactor central to cellular energy metabolism. Human trials show NR can raise blood NAD+; broader outcome benefits are still being studied.

Randomised human trial ↗

06 · Polyphenol

Resveratrol

A plant polyphenol studied across many areas of human health. Reviews describe promising signals, while emphasising that larger, higher-quality clinical trials are needed.

Clinical-trial review ↗
B3B6B12

07 · Essential vitamins

B vitamins

B6 supports more than 100 enzyme reactions involved in metabolism. B12 has a role in energy metabolism; extra B12 does not enhance performance when nutritional status is already sufficient.

NIH fact sheet ↗
